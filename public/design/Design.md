## Brand & Style
The brand personality embodies dark gothic romanticism paired with an unapologetic, rebellious edge. It balances raw street tattoo culture with feminine tenderness, intimate craftsmanship, and curated mystery. Tailored for clients seeking fine-line, lettering, script, and bespoke illustrative ink in Fürth/Nürnberg, the experience should evoke intimacy, artistic exclusivity, and fearless personal expression.

The design system blends **High-Contrast Dark Noir** with **Tactile Grunge & Romantic Brutalism**:
- Deep midnight canvas layers accented with luminous, electric hot pinks and soft blush glows.
- Expressive vintage typography contrasted against ultra-legible, modern editorial body fonts.
- Hand-rendered romantic iconography (four-point sparkles, hearts, grunge splatters, fine hairline frames) that anchors the gritty boutique studio identity into sleek, functional digital interactions.

## Colors
The color foundation is built on an absolute black canvas (`#08080A`) layered with subtle soot tiers (`#121214`, `#1A1A1E`) to give dark ink photography supreme contrast. 

- **Primary (`#FF4081`):** Electric neon pink used for critical callouts, interactive states, selected chips, and glowing accent strokes.
- **Secondary (`#F8B4D9`):** Soft vintage blush pink used for delicate hairline borders, badges, secondary icons, and romantic embellishments.
- **Tertiary (`#B388FF`):** Deep mystical lilac used selectively for status accents, studio announcements, or subtle duotone ambient highlights.
- **Neutral (`#121214` / `#F5F5F7`):** Foundation soot surfaces paired with bone/pure white high-contrast text layers for effortless legibility.

Never tint body backgrounds with heavy pinks; keep the studio environment velvety black to highlight tattoo skin tones and stippled artwork.

## Typography
Typography creates a tension between dark romance and modern booking efficiency.

- **Headline & Display (`Bodoni Moda`):** High-contrast editorial serifs evoke the precision, vertical drama, and classic elegance of old-world lettering and blackletter heritage without sacrificing mobile clarity. Used in uppercase for section banners or title cards, often accompanied by centered star sparkle symbols (`✦`) or subtle tracking.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, crisp, geometric sans-serif that balances the theatrical headlines with approachable readability for aftercare guidelines, booking forms, and consultation instructions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid system on desktop, an 8-column layout on tablet, and a single-to-two-column layout on mobile. Outer screen margins breathe generously to mimic luxury art catalogues.

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 16px lateral padding with compact 8px to 16px component stacking to facilitate rapid booking, flash tattoo viewing, and intake steps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 32px lateral margins with modular 2-column card arrays for portfolio grids and aftercare steps.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Centered max-width container (1280px) flanked by deep black gutters, allowing flash pieces, pricing modules, and consultation calendars to sit gracefully with breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced through tonal soot stacking, hairline illuminated borders, and diffuse neon pink back-glows rather than murky drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Pure jet black `#08080A`.
- **Card & Container Layer (Level 1):** Midnight charcoal `#121214` bordered by a 1px solid stroke of `#24242A` or `#FF4081` at 20% opacity.
- **Interactive & Raised Floating Panels (Level 2):** Elevated `#1A1A20` with a sharp hairline border of `#FF4081` (40% opacity) accompanied by an ambient neon halo: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 64, 129, 0.22)`.
- **Overlay & Modal Sheet (Level 3):** Frosted charcoal `#0E0E12` with 80% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bound by a crisp 1px `#F8B4D9` rim at 30% opacity.

## Shapes
A "Soft" roundedness (`roundedness: 1` / 4px base radius) preserves the sharp, structured edge of gothic architecture and tattoo stencils while avoiding harsh brutalist ergonomics:

- **Buttons & Inputs:** Subtly rounded at 4px (`0.25rem`) to remain architectural and clean.
- **Featured Badges & Story Chips:** Rounded-full (pill-shaped) for contrast against rectangular cards.
- **Images & Frame Holders:** 8px (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem`) with 1px inner pink or charcoal borders, evoking dark framing mats.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** High-voltage hot pink (`#FF4081`) background, deep black text (`#0A0A0C`), bold geometric weight, 4px border radius. On hover: subtle neon glow `0 0 16px rgba(255, 64, 129, 0.6)` with light scale lift (1.02).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1px solid `#F8B4D9` border and `#F8B4D9` text. Hover transitions to `#FF4081` border with a 10% translucent pink fill.
- **Icon / Accent Button:** Black circle with a delicate 1px pink hairline, housing sparkling stars (`✦`), hearts, or back arrows.

### Chips & Badges
- **Booking / Style Tags:** Pill-shaped capsules with deep soot background (`#16161C`), 1px outline in `#FF4081` at 30%, accompanied by an electric pink label.
- **Aftercare Pill Badges:** Pale pink pill ribbons (`#F8B4D9`) displaying capitalized high-contrast black serif lettering for step milestones.

### Cards & Flash Showcases
- **Tattoo Flash Card:** Midnight slate background, 1px perimeter line in `#26262E` that transitions to `#FF4081` on focus/hover. Embedded images feature top corner star-sparkle accents and delicate price/time badges pinned to the bottom.
- **Warning / Notice Card:** Distressed or brushed inner border with neon pink stroke, framed with miniature heart bullets (`♥`) and italicized serif advisory text.

### Inputs & Booking Fields
- **Text Inputs & Date Pickers:** Deep soot surface (`#121214`), light bone text (`#F5F5F7`), bottom hairline border or 1px perimeter in `#2A2A32`. On active focus: border transitions to vibrant `#FF4081` with an inner 2px faint glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Angular 4px squares/circles in `#18181F` border; checked state floods with `#FF4081` featuring a crisp white heart or checkmark glyph.

### Lists & Aftercare Guides
- List items feature custom pink bullet marks (such as stippled hearts `♥` or four-point stars `✦`) spaced 12px from secondary bone-colored typography.
